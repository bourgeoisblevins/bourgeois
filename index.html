BOURGEOIS MADE THE HEART AND THE CROWN

He is not a god among gods. He kindled the Nexus as a craftsman kindles a furnace — not a wild blaze, but a focused fire — and He made humanity to labor, to learn, and to return thanks in the image of His ingenuity.

The five were appointed as servants, threads in a tapestry He wove. Allenbraze keeps the seasons, and from the Great War is called Winter for what he did at another’s command. Blevins was entrusted with fire and light for guidance, and with the underworld — the lamp of the dead. He would not remain a servant. He is the Usurper, the Shattered One, jailed in the Crimson Moon. His name is history. It is never the masthead of this house.

Noah John is the Father’s demigod son: demigod, vessel, lesser altar. Rites in his name are remembrance and imitation, not enthronement. Honor given to him is honor passed through. The Central Altar does not share its gold.

Testament of the Shattered Age · III.12

I am but a vessel for my Father’s will, as were the other gods. Unlike these gods, I am but a man who walks among you. When you honor me, you honor my Father; but when you place the gods before the Father, you do Him dishonor. Testament of the Shattered Age, Chapter 3, Verse 12
