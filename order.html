Tenets

THE COIL THAT HOLDS

01

DEVOTION TO BOURGEOIS

The Creator and supreme deity, whose light illuminates the path to unity and order. Let no god, however beloved, eclipse His glory. Kneel only before the Father.

02

REVERENCE FOR THE NEXUS

The divine gift that sustains reality and the source of the Father's focused will. Guard the knowledge of it. Guard the altars that serve as its earthly echo.

03

HARMONY AMONG THE GODS

Honor each according to the office the Father appointed. Keep their festivals. Do not set them against one another, and do not set them against Him. Blevins is not restored to the lesser gold.

04

DISCIPLINE AND KNOWLEDGE

Enlightenment is earned by study, meditation, and service. Labor is worship. Mastery, rightly ordered, is the image of the Father's ingenuity working through mortal fingers.

05

DEFIANCE OF CHAOS

Rebellion against the Father's design is not freedom. It is unmaking. The Usurper is the caution. The coil is the answer.

Hierarchy

THE HOUSE IS A COIL

Each turn of the body keeps the next from sliding. Kick a rung and the climbers fall.

  1. High Priest of the Nexus

    Communes with Bourgeois and interprets His will. Does not create the law. When communion is silent, he waits.

  2. Council of Elders

    Senior priests who oversee regional temples and advise the High Priest on divine matters.

  3. Temple Priests

    Local leaders who perform ceremonies, guide communities, and keep the sacred altars.

  4. Monks

    Scholars and caretakers. They copy the Codices, study the Secrets of the Shattered as a wound is studied, and train initiates. Noah John was raised among them.

  5. Devotees

    Lay followers who labor, offer, and raise the next generation. The household is a small temple.

The lesser altar

NOAH JOHN

Demigod. The Father's demigod son. Vessel. These three sit together without a fight if you will let them.

He was born of flesh. His soul was molded by the Father's hand. Unlike the appointed gods, he is a man who walks among the people. The Father made of him a vessel and a demigod, not to sit upon the gold, but to restore balance to a world sliding back toward the waste.

Rites in his name are rites of remembrance and imitation, not of enthronement. A vessel does not pour himself into the cup and then drink. When you honor him, you honor his Father. When you place him — or any god — before the Father, you do the Father dishonor.

I am but a vessel for my Father's will, as were the other gods. Unlike these gods, I am but a man who walks among you. When you honor me, you honor my Father; but when you place the gods before the Father, you do Him dishonor. Testament of the Shattered Age, Chapter 3, Verse 12

After the victory he ascended. Ryan, plainsman and general, was given the keeping of the Order — not as a second vessel, but as a keeper who refused to become the thing Noah would not become.

Rites

GOLDEN LIGHTS FROM THE ALTARS

HYMNS OF CREATION

Every sunrise. Thanks for life and light. The monks lead. The people answer. The hours that follow are the labor that makes the chant honest.

RITUAL OF ILLUMINATION

At sunset, and annually for the Nexus. Golden candles in the shape of the Serpent's Eye. Desert incense. Offerings of grain, oil, and crafted goods — the fruit of labor.

HIGH COMMUNION

Innermost chamber. The High Priest kneels and asks to be a vessel. A golden glow is often seen. What is received is written by monks and delivered as edict — never a private traffic.

ASCENSION AND DEDICATION

Priests are anointed. Men, once, take the razor and then never again: hair a crown of faith, beard a symbol of wisdom. Women cover the head as the earth is clothed by the sky. The body is a living testament.

FIRST FLAME, HARVEST, BINDING

Bonfires for the kindling of the Nexus. First fruits blessed before gathering. Marriage under golden bands and a shared candle. The Father's ordinary is not cancelled by His emergency.

CHANT OF THE GUARDIANS

Warriors before battle. The Father first, Noah as example, Roldwin as servant. If that order is inverted, a battle has already been lost that the enemy did not need to attend.

Temples

ZIGGURAT, NOT THEATRE

Temples are monumental: ziggurats and pyramids of gold, obsidian, and carving. Interiors are labyrinthine, a teaching in stone — creation is complex, and the path to the innermost chamber is not a straight road for the careless. Each house a Central Nexus Altar of radiant gold, and smaller altars in its shadow. Gold illuminates. It does not flood the walls.

Robes of the priesthood are gold, trimmed in crimson. Crimson is punctuation: robe-trim and ritual. Never a wash. Ember is reserved for the Moon and for war.
