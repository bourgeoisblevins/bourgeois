The Codices of the Serpent's Light

TWO BOOKSONE COIL

The Order's scripture is a two-part work. The Ancient Doctrine holds the Codex of the Eternal Light as a book inside it. The Testament of the Shattered Age is Noah's teaching after the war. Secrets of the Shattered is studied in the monasteries. It is not a third Codex.

The collection

THE ANCIENT DOCTRINE

Pre-Shattering. Written by early monks and priests at the height of the Order. Creation, the Divine Mandates, the offices of the gods, the rites.

Inside that collection sits a book: the Codex of the Eternal Light. It is not another name for the whole first codex. It is not a third scripture. Verses that name Eternal Light are verses from that inner book.

The Golden Father is the light that casts no shadow, the fire that does not burn, and the breath that fills all living things. He speaks through the Nexus, and His word is the law that binds creation. Obey Him, for in His wisdom lies eternal harmony. Codex of the Eternal Light, Chapter 1, Verse 8
The gods are threads in the great tapestry, each woven by the Father's hand. They are not to be worshipped above Him but alongside Him, for they are His servants, just as you are. Honor the gods, but kneel only before the Father. Codex of the Eternal Light, Chapter 4, Verse 10
The Nexus is the heart of creation, the Father's gift to bind the world together. Through it, His voice can be heard, and His will made known. Guard it with your lives, for without it, all shall fall into nothingness. Codex of the Eternal Light, Chapter 2, Verse 6

After the war

THE TESTAMENT OF THE SHATTERED AGE

Compiled after the defeat of the Blevins. History of the Great War. Teachings of Noah John, written primarily by Ryan and the other disciples. Authenticated by the priesthood as an extension of the Father's will.

I am but a vessel for my Father's will, as were the other gods. Unlike these gods, I am but a man who walks among you. When you honor me, you honor my Father; but when you place the gods before the Father, you do Him dishonor. Testament of the Shattered Age, Chapter 3, Verse 12
The Usurper sought to unmake the Father's design, casting the world into chaos. Beware the lies of the Shattered One and his followers, for they offer power but bring only ruin. Hold fast to the light, and the Father will protect you. Testament of the Shattered Age, Chapter 5, Verse 9
Though I was born of flesh, my soul was molded by the Father's hand. My victories are not my own, but His, for it was His strength that carried me. Follow not me, but the path He has set before you, and you shall know peace. Testament of the Shattered Age, Chapter 8, Verse 11

Studied, not enthroned

SECRETS OF THE SHATTERED

A holy text written by survivors of the Cult of the Crimson Moon. Jeriah is among its authors. Order monasteries already study it as a wound is studied: to know the enemy's shape, not to restore him to the altar.

It does not sit inside the Codices. It is not a third Codex. Keep it labeled. Keep it separate.
