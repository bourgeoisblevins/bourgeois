The making

HE NAMED ITAND IT WAS BOUND

Before light there was not darkness as mortals know darkness, but the unshaped waste. Bourgeois spoke, and His word was law. He kindled the Nexus as a furnace rather than a wild blaze, appointed the gods as servants, and made humanity the crown of creation — a steward’s crown, worn, not owned.

Creation

THE NEXUS AND THE CROWN

The world is not an accident, and the gods are not its authors. Bourgeois is the author. The Nexus is His gift. The gods are His instruments. Humanity is His steward.

He did not wrestle the waste as a warrior wrestles a foe. He named it, and in the naming it was bound. Seeing that limitless power unfocused would unmake as readily as it made, He fashioned the Nexus — a furnace, not a blaze. Through it His will is useful to the world. Through it His voice may be heard.

Then He made humanity, not as beasts that only feed nor as stones that only stand. He made them to labor, to learn, to keep order, and to return thanks. In their hands He placed craft. In their hearts, the hunger for enlightenment. Life, nature, and human ingenuity are sacred reflections of His will. The world was not given as spoil. It was given as a trust.

Codex of the Eternal Light · II.6

The Nexus is the heart of creation, the Father's gift to bind the world together. Through it, His voice can be heard, and His will made known. Guard it with your lives, for without it, all shall fall into nothingness. Codex of the Eternal Light, Chapter 2, Verse 6

The pantheon

HONOR THE GODS. KNEEL ONLY BEFORE THE FATHER.

They are threads, each with a color, each with a tension, none permitted to fray the cloth. Their festivals are kept. Their glory returns to the One who wove them.

The source · not an office

BOURGEOIS

The Golden Father, architect of existence. He created the Nexus and humanity. He designed the pantheon and appointed their authorities. Temples raise a Central Nexus Altar of radiant gold to Him alone. He is not one god among the five. He is the hand.

Stone and war

ROLDWIN

The weight that holds the wall, and the strength that defends it. Honored by masons and by those who take up arms in a just defense. Warriors invoke him for the line. The Father is named first.

Sea, rain, cleansing

LAZANMAI

The return of the tide and the washing of what has been soiled. The bowls of renewal are his office, borrowed for the Father's covenant. The sea is his charge. The law is the Father's.

Seasons · called Winter from the war

ALLENBRAZE

Appointed over the seasons: winter, harvest, and the preservation of store. Farmers keep his festival. From the Great War he is called Winter, for at the Blevins' command he froze Sakura. The office remains the Father's. The act is recorded. Hatred is not permitted to write a sixth scripture.

Thunder, wind, punishment

TANNAIRIUS

The voice that does not flatter and the blast that does not ask permission. Invoked when a wrong has grown too large for the quiet word, and always in fear, because the wind does not always stop at the house of the guilty.

Fallen · other · not an office of this house

BLEVINS

He was appointed over fire and light for guidance, and over the underworld — the lamp that led souls through the cycle of return. He is not restored to current Order worship. He rebelled, sought the Nexus to finish the Deep, and was shattered. He waits in the Crimson Moon. He is antagonist, never chrome, never the mark of this house. The War chapter keeps his record.

Underworld and Usurper belong to War.

Stewardship

HUMANITY IS A CROWN, NOT A HEAD

A crown is worn. It is not the owner. The Father designed humankind to reflect His ingenuity and purpose. That reflection is a duty. When we invent, we invent as stewards. When we rule, we rule as stewards. When we fight, we fight as stewards.

The gods aid the steward. They do not replace him. Roldwin will not hold the wall if no one stacks the stones. Allenbraze will not keep the jar if no one seals it. Defiance of chaos is stewardship in arms. Chaos is not a rival creator. It is the unmaking.
